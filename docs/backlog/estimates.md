# Estimate calibration

Read whenever you change a record (its effort is reassessed when scope, dependencies, implementation or verification change), and before you close a step or re-estimate open steps. Part of the [backlog rules](../BACKLOG-RULES.md).

## Effort

- Effort estimates remaining work, including investigation, implementation and verification: `🟢 Small` (up to one focused day), `🟡 Medium` (two to three days), `🔴 Large` (four or more days). Work estimated in agent hours is sized by its remaining agent hours, owner time apart (F-063): `🟢 Small` up to 1 h, `🟡 Medium` up to 4 h, `🔴 Large` up to 8 h, `🟣 Very large` more; the generated ledger sizes each open step and, when all its open steps are in hours, the record, so the size falls as the work is done. Colour always accompanies the word. Record a dated rationale, assumptions and uncertainty in details; an optional range such as 2–4 hours or 1–2 days can make that estimate more useful. Reassess effort when scope, dependencies, implementation or verification changes, and review related records. Keep pending acceptance in remaining effort. Status and verification evidence describe progress; completion percentages are removed from active records. Model tokens/cost are separate usage or budget measurements/estimates, not a measure of remaining project work.

## Estimate calibration

**Estimate calibration.** A verified or closed step has no remaining effort: set its `agentHours`, `ownerHours` and `days` to 0, or `check:work` refuses it (B-043). When a step closes, record what it took next to its baseline and set `effort.reviewed` to the close date: `effort.actualAgentHours` (or `effort.actualDays` for a step still in days) with `effort.actualSource`. For a step in agent hours, an actual is the agent's elapsed time from starting the step to the merge of its last PR, reviews and CI waits included and pauses left out (time the agent could not run, such as a usage-limit stop or the owner away), the same quantity the estimate predicts: `measured` when it comes from the work-usage effective elapsed time of the jobs linked to the task, which leaves out the pauses recorded with `work-usage pause`, `stated` when it is the agent's or owner's own account. For a step still in days, `measured` is the active effort of the linked jobs in hours divided by `workItems.estimation.hoursPerDay` (default 6), never wall-clock time with waits. A stated figure is never shown as measured. When the time is not known (for example a fresh session that cannot tell when the step started), record no actual and write `No actual: <reason>` in the basis: never invent a figure. A cancelled or superseded step records no actual effort; a zero actual is not a sample. While a step is in progress (not while it is only planned), the actual may hold the effort spent so far; the steps table shows Effort (the size of the remaining agent time), Estimate (the latest whole-step estimate), Actual and Remaining side by side (a step without a recorded actual shows `0 h` or `0 days` with no source; a recorded zero shows its source, and the total row says how many steps recorded one); the baseline stays in the task file for calibration and is not shown (F-063 retires 6.2.0's `(planned …)` suffix), a step back in `planned` drops its actual, and only closed steps are calibration samples. `node tools/work-items.mjs calibrate` compares actual with baseline for closed steps, overall and by size, source, unit and kind (`bug` for B- steps, `feature` for F- steps; the same formula for both), with sample counts; it never rewrites an estimate. `check:freshness` asks for a re-estimation when, among steps closed since `workItems.estimation.since`, at least `minSamples` (default 5) have a median actual/baseline outside 1/`ratio`–`ratio` (default 1.5), overall or, when both kinds closed steps, within one kind on its own, or one step is `outlier` (default 3) times off either way. Then revise the remaining estimate (`agentHours` with `estimateAgentHours`, or `days` for a step still in days) of the open steps with a dated basis citing the calibration (start from each step's current estimate and revise the steps that resemble the samples that missed; never multiply every open step by one ratio), keep every baseline, propose a material increase to the owner as [feature plans](feature-plans.md) says, and set `since` to the day of the re-estimation: only steps closed after it count, so the same samples do not trigger again. `workItems.estimation: false` turns the reminder off.
