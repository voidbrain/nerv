## Reading

- **For code, qwen3-coder:30b:** 4/4 coding and 4/6 code-review in seconds, without thinking; both gemma4 models run out of reply on coding.
- **gemma4:12b is as good or better than gemma4:26b for NERV's planner and embedded work:** planner 18/36 against 17/36, embedded 3/4 against 2/4, cad-3d 10/10 against a first case that never ends. Night and evening agree, so the shared, loaded Mac was not the cause.

- **Same quality where both finish:** coding, long-context, structured output, tool use, bilingual, refusal and Italian at 4,096 give the same passes and the same failures.
- **Faster:** gemma4:26b generates about 40 to 50% faster and finishes most cases sooner (coding about 25% sooner).
- **Runs out of budget more often:** with thinking on, gemma4:26b writes much longer replies on some tasks (`cad-3d` slicer, `logic-math` race-order, `italian-quality` summary) and hits the 2,048 cap or the 30 s case where gemma4:12b does not. On `cad-3d` and `logic-math` gemma4:12b is better under NERV's current limits.
- **Memory:** about 16 GB resident against 8 GB; with the Parallels VM up, free memory fell to 19% and swap to 28 GB during the evening runs.
