## 4. Handoff to `F-006-T12` (Placement Report)

- **Mac (`NERV-01`) inference**: `gemma4:12b` and `gemma4:26b` both hit 5/5 on Casper (`gemma4:26b` is ~29% faster at 6.8 s avg); no local model yet qualifies for Misato (all scored ≤ 3/5 on `misato-planner-v2`, consistently failing `exact-reminder` and `correct-rejected-time`).
- **Mini PC (`NERV-02`, Tabgha) routing**: `models.remote` to `NERV-01` (`gemma4:26b`) preserves full Neo Tokyo-3 accuracy (`5/5` Casper, `3/5` Misato) with +0.3–1.1 s LAN latency and 4 ms fail-fast when `NERV-01` is asleep/unreachable. Local CPU fallback (`qwen2.5:3b`) is viable only for degraded Casper intent voting (`4/5`, ~10 s avg), not Misato planning (`5/5` 30 s timeouts).
- **Open F-078 follow-up**: `F-078-T08` retains 9 pending cases (`refusal-permission`, `tool-use`) awaiting owner budget/timeout decision.
