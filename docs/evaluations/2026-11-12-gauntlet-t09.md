## The time limit on 2026-11-12

The earlier diagnosis (the 840 s pack limit was reached) was not the cause for the last seven cases. The Ollama log for the 30 s attempt shows the model load took about 7 s inside the first case, then generation at about 24 tokens per second, still thinking at about 540 tokens when the call was cancelled at 30 s. At that rate the profile's 2,048-token allowance needs up to about 85 s, so a 30 s case limit cannot hold a long thinking reply on this machine. With 90 s, the same case took 54 s and 1,130 tokens; the other six took 6–13 s.

The 90 s limit was passed with `gauntlet:all --limits=FILE` for this run only; NERV's default model limits (Gauntlet's day limits, 30 s per case) are unchanged.
