## Observed load trial

Command: `node --test test/process-client.test.ts test/history.test.ts test/runtime-load.test.ts test/dashboard-poll.test.ts`.

Local host: macOS, Node v26.12.0. Seed: 10,000 delivered reminders and inbox entries; 100 bounded history reads; eight concurrent CLI writer loops continued across the due window (asserted using operation intervals), with 16 CLI operations in the observed run. Brain was CPU-stalled, then killed before a second due reminder.

Observed standalone run: page p95 10 ms, maximum 43 ms; slowest CLI operation 1,208 ms; persisted delivery timestamp 11 ms after its due time. The executable regression allows up to 5,000 ms and prints every run's own measurements; CI uses Node 24 on Linux/Windows, so those results may differ. These numbers describe this workload and machine only. The previous preliminary trial did not prove overlapping writes; the sustained-writer trial supersedes it.

## Limits

Same-user children are workload boundaries, not security sandboxes. Long external transactions can block synchronous SQLite until its 5 s busy timeout; a busy host, large due backlog, sleep or shutdown can delay delivery. Local CLI exports and the daily brief may aggregate pages in coordinator memory; web handlers keep queries and displayed pages bounded. History cursors are local rowids for browsing, not durable restore checkpoints; native notification delivery retains its separate UUID-validated inbox checkpoint. A saturated stalled Brain stays saturated until it replies or disconnects; timeout never authorizes replay of an uncertain effect. Remote workers, global connection budgets and worker fencing remain F-014/F-005.

Remote CI and independent review are recorded on F-013 in the backlog/archive. This report does not establish native permissions, phone receipt or target-host login/logout acceptance.
