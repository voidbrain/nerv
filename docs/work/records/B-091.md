<summary>Next action, estimates and evidence</summary>

**2026-11-25 cause named:** the failing statement was not the journal write but the stop step's own poll: `waitFor` runs `host.inspect()`, which opened the live `~/.nerv/nerv.sqlite` read-only with no busy handler (`src/node-lifecycle-windows.ts`, `PRAGMA user_version`), and its error ends the step at `stop:started`. Reproduced on Tabgha in a temporary folder (Node 24.22.2): a reader polling a WAL database while its writer is killed (as `taskkill /F` ends `serve`) failed 16 times in 40 cycles with `disk I/O error` (errcode 1546, `SQLITE_IOERR_TRUNCATE`) and 2,747 times with `SQLITE_READONLY_ROLLBACK`; with a 5 s busy timeout, none. Same cause class as B-126 (an open without a busy timeout), in other code. Fix: `liveDatabase()` opens every lifecycle read of the live database with `timeout: 5000` (Windows, macOS and Linux inspect, update admission, release and restore checks, macOS backup); the failing-first test in `test/windows-service-lifecycle.test.ts` holds the live database during `inspect()` and failed with `database is locked` before the fix. 2026-11-25 reconciliation: [#742](https://github.com/voidbrain/nerv/pull/742) merged on green CI (main `2627d483`). Next: once NERV-02 (Tabgha, at `1c407fb8`, before the fix) is on `2627d483` or later, a fenced stop on Tabgha from its desktop session completes its receipt (the Check).
