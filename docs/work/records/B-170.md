<summary>Next action, estimates and evidence</summary>

**Root cause (evidence so far):** the service accepts and records a valid receipt (tests and the bogus-receipt probe), and the worker's click handler sends it; since no tap on two installs ever arrived, the request is lost on the phone. WebKit has open reports that match: `notificationclick` not firing for Home Screen apps ([WebKit 268797](https://bugs.webkit.org/show_bug.cgi?id=268797)), the click handled in a separate process before the page loads ([252544](https://bugs.webkit.org/show_bug.cgi?id=252544)), `openWindow` landing on the app's root ([263687](https://bugs.webkit.org/show_bug.cgi?id=263687)). Which of these applies is not proven from the Mac; T02's log line (`push: opened … via worker|page`) shows it at the retest. The fix does not depend on which.

**Plan:** B-170-T01 failing tests (worker request lost, notification data lost, no click event at all, `via` and the log line, device-bound subscriptions, the done action). B-170-T02 the opened page reports the tap: the worker puts the receipt in the notification's `tag` and in Cache Storage (`nerv-push-pending`) after showing it, opens `/dashboard/?opened=<receipt>`; the page (`live.js`, before sign-in, no bearer) reports `?opened=` and, on load and whenever it becomes visible, every kept receipt whose notification is no longer shown; `POST /api/push/opened` takes an optional `via` and logs each outcome without the receipt. Assumption (cheap to undo, stated here and in the PR): a notification the owner removed from Notification Centre and then opened the app counts as seen, like a tap; one still shown never counts. B-170-T03 a subscription made by a paired device carries its device id: the device's next subscription replaces it and revoking the device removes it (404/410 from the push service already removes one, F-004-T05). B-170-T04 Done for delivered reminders: Coming up lists reminders delivered in the last 24 h and not done, each with **Done**; `POST /api/reminders/<id>/done` sets `reminders.done` and the item's `read_at` once. B-170-T05 procedure and area notes. B-170-T06 owner retest. The workspace app (Angular, `workspace-sw.js`) keeps the worker-only report for now: the owner's push app is the dashboard.

**Estimates:** Medium (5.5 h agent), estimated 2026-11-26: T01 1 h, T02 1.5 h, T03 1 h, T04 1.5 h, T05 0.5 h; T06 owner 0.5 h. Not a measurement.
