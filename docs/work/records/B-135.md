### B-135

**Update now on a busy Mac is refused as an ownership problem when NERV's health check is slow**

| Signal | Priority | Status | Effort |
|:---:|---|---|---|
| 🟡 | 🔶 P2 | Implemented | 🟢 Small |

🧑 **→ Human action required: Procedure**<br />After the update reaches the service, press Update now in the dashboard while the Mac is busy (for example during a full `npm test` in another checkout): the update either goes through, or, if NERV is slower than 2 × 5 s, the refusal reads "NERV did not answer its health check within 5.0 s (2 attempts, … s in all; the machine may be busy) … Nothing was stopped; try again in a minute." and never "Service or runtime ownership is unknown or belongs elsewhere.".
